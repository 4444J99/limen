  stable owner indexes. No personal-data deletion or lossy deduplication occurs without the
  declared two-copy/restore predicate.
- All corrections are additive. They do not detour from the campaign supervisor, strict Omega,
  semantic convergence, prospective proof, or two-pass settlement.
