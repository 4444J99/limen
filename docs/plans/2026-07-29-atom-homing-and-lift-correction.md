# α→ω — home the unhomed, correct the lifting rows

Successor arc to `2026-07-25-iceberg-corpus-harvest-shared-substrate.md`, whose Ω fixed point
closed on 2026-07-26 (541 threads drained to 4,099 atoms, all five predicates green). That closed
the **drain**. It did not close the **product of the drain**.

## Context — why this arc exists

Two measurements the prior closeout could not see:

1. **Only 11% of the harvest was homed.** Of 4,099 atoms in 8 kinds, exactly one kind —
   `projects-to-start` — reached a git-tracked owner (165 `IRF-BRC` rows in the private
   `organvm/organvm-corpvs-testamentvm`). **3,658 atoms across 7 kinds had no declared owner at
   all**, resting in a store `institutio/governance/corpora.yaml` declares `remote: none`
   ("load-bearing … why the publish gate can never reach it") with every brainstorm corpus
   `redacted: false` ⇒ "never leaves its store". An un-homed atom is a vacuum, and Rule #1 says a
   vacuum is never a resting state.
2. **Four rows of `institutio/governance/convergence.yaml` misdescribe the code.** Declared data
   that contradicts ground truth is worse than a vacuum: it routes work at the wrong target.

A third fact arrived mid-execution and sharpened the first: **the corpus has been cold-archived off
this host.** The laptop-evacuation lane moved `_conversations-private` on 2026-07-27 under a
verified two-copy receipt (1,763 files / 428 MB; `Archive4T` + `T7Recovery`; both
`restore.passed: true`; local copy purged against a purge receipt). Custody is sound — the corpus is
safe and restorable. But it means the drain's entire 4,099-atom result now lives *only* on offline
volumes, with no committed projection in git. That is the Rule #2 breach ("nothing local-only")
realized rather than hypothetical, and it is what makes the census artifact below load-bearing
rather than decorative.

## The constraint that shapes everything

Atom **statement text may never enter the public `organvm/limen` tree.** Homing is therefore
**distillation, not transfer**: 518 `functionality-to-repeat` atoms become a handful of `IF-*`
entries; 877 `decisions` do not become 877 rows anywhere. What crosses into the public tree is a
generalization, a count, or an id — never a statement. Statements stay in the `remote: none` store;
durable private records go to the private IRF repo.

## Phase α — the homing spine — **LANDED**

Branch `feat/atom-homing-registry`. No new substrate: this instantiates two shipped patterns —
`personal-facts.yaml` + `check-personal-facts.py` (the class→home→verify→consumers registry with
lettered checks) and `prompt-atom-ledger.py` (private corpus → redacted public control view).

- **`institutio/governance/atom-homing.yaml`** — one row per atom kind:
  `{home, home_class: public|private|broker, unit: cluster|stream|atom, admits, verify, consumers,
  residue_baseline, owner_of_record, note}`, plus an optional `deferred` block. The sixth VIGILIA
  axis beside GATES / SENSORS / PARAMETERS / CORPORA / CONVERGENCE.
- **`institutio/governance/atom-census.yaml`** — the statement-free projection: counts, kinds, ids.
  Seeded from the 2026-07-26 measurement with the evacuation receipt as its provenance anchor, and
  regenerated by `brainstorm-harvest.py --census` whenever the store is mounted. Clock-free by
  design so reruns are byte-idempotent.
- **`institutio/governance/atom-residue-baseline.txt`** — the monotonic ceiling. Splitting it out
  means shrinking residue is a deliberate two-file edit and *raising* it is a visible diff.
- **`scripts/check-atom-homing.py`** — checks **A** schema · **B** home resolves (host-aware;
  public homes bind everywhere, private homes reuse `check-convergence`'s resolver) · **C**
  completeness against the committed census, store-free · **D** leak (no extract signature and no
  literal atom id in the public tree — the executable form of `redacted: false`) · **E** residue
  ratchet · **F** consumers derive · **G** anti-fake (a wholly-deferred kind is RED).
- **`scripts/brainstorm-harvest.py`** — `ATOM_KINDS` now *derives* from the registry (check F holds
  it there), and `--census` emits the projection, refusing to write zeros when the store is absent.
- **`institutio/governance/gates.yaml`** — one `check-atom-homing` row, `ci_job: pr-gate.yml:pr-gate`.

Current predicate output: `8 kinds homed, 4099 of 4099 atoms residual, checks A-G clean`. The
residue is seeded at the full population deliberately — a ratchet seeded high is safe; one seeded
low silently forgives real residue.
