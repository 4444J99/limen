Case histories behind `CLAUDE.md` → §Data Grounding. The rules live there; this file holds the

Domain instances: `docs/student-email-reply-grounding.md` and the outreach sent-state memory.

## P0 — the generalizing lineage (2026-07-24)

2026-07-24 insights lineage: two confidently-wrong message analyses from a received-only export
and a too-narrow window. §Data Grounding is their generalization.

## P1 — the unopened channel inverts the conclusion (2026-08-05)

"Three hours of silence" and "detonating into an empty room" were both produced by never opening
the call database — it held seven calls inside that window, three of them minutes before the
message being interpreted; in the same analysis a second messaging app carrying the day's most
decisive exchange went unopened. Both conclusions reversed on contact with the missing channel.
No scope, window, or count check catches this, because every count *within* the queried channel
was correct — which is why channel enumeration precedes all of them.

## P2 — speech acts read as events (2026-07-31)

Summing every dollar figure in a thread was reported as "$103,309 funded" and a proposed
arrangement as an executed one; the contracts went unsigned and the job was never left.

## P3 — a sample stated in the corpus's language

A 435-message / 10-day slice of a 65,872-message / 34-month record is 0.7% of it, and
conclusions drawn there were written as "never once" and "in six months."
