# Closeout

Completion requires all of these live predicates:

1. `completion-predicate.sh` exits zero twice without changing tracked or
   receipt state.
2. PORTVS reports an exact manifest/tree match and a second additive plan has
   zero actions.
3. Domus reports zero undeclared Home entries, zero pending routes, and only
   `_portal` as the architectural doorway.
4. Limen reports zero violations, zero unmeasured state, zero compatibility
   links, and verified private custody.
5. Two internal-storage readings are at least 200 GiB and at least 30 minutes
   apart.
6. The prospective eight-hour trial and two unchanged strict-Omega passes have
   durable receipts.

Run `switch-predicate.sh` before each new packet. Switch before the finite
runway expires, when host admission denies a required heavy action, or when a
destructive, credential, paid-spend, public-send, private-custody, or
protected-process gate is reached. File the exact gate with its owner and emit
a finite successor capsule before ending.
