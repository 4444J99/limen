## Vocabulary change and counterexamples

[V5](vocabulary-v5.json) retains all nineteen V4 operation contracts unchanged and adds
`/open <artifact> --app <application>`. The artifact may be inherited when it is unambiguous;
an explicitly requested viewer must be retained. Opening does not authorize editing, signing,
attaching or sending. Completion requires observing the requested display state, or reporting
that it remains unobserved. Applying a selected change and displaying an existing artifact
remain different operations.

The sequence also tests several earlier assumptions:

- A rhetorical question can request a process or artifact repair; mapping its words to explain
  would miss the action expected at positions 4, 7 and 10.
- An external review's clean disposition is evidence. It is not the operator's authorization
  for subsequent action.
- A long pasted report cannot become a bare continue command: its contents and authority
  remain material, and the next direct correction expressly narrows execution.
- The later draft-only correction governs subsequent activity; it does not retroactively grant
  authority to earlier actions.
- An explicit application argument changes the display requirement even when the artifact
  stays the same.

This is one-coder contextual analysis, not independent substitution validation. Nine new case
dispositions are kept separate from earlier file-based and recovered-segment denominators
until source-origin overlap is resolved. The source is not copied into the raw comparison:
its abstract sequence is shown here, and the existing comparison's current glossary reflects V5.
