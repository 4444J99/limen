## T01: selection, synthesis and execution

Source lines: 15 (opener), 34, 323, 579. Positions 1 and 2 share one native
turn and final reply. Position 2 is /choose with its clarification-question
binding and all-six scope. Position 3 combines /review and /plan: preserve its
new evidence distinctions, root-to-leaf priority and canonical-source payload.
Position 4 is /continue --plan, retaining reuse, preservation, deployment and
consumer-acceptance requirements. Its reply reports partial implementation,
tests and publication while explicitly leaving deployment and acceptance open.
Executing the plan is therefore not evidence the requested outcome completed.

## T02: relayed evidence is not a fresh approval

Source lines: 10 (opener), 713, 717. Positions 2 and 3 share a native turn
and final reply. Both are /context --kind peer-report with their actual payloads.
The latter quotes another assistant asking permission for downloads. Relaying
that question does not establish the human answered it. The historical reply
reports a download and corrected counts; this study neither verifies that
external action nor infers its authorization from the quoted permission request.
Embedded goal frames remain separate from the three retained prompt rows.

## T03: supplied results change the next predicate

## T04: shorthand needs the pending-step binding

Source lines: 10 (opener), 86, 99, 273, 283, 293, 303, 313, 344, 383,
429, 505, 535, 545, 563, 573, 628. Position 2 is /correct with the capability
claim. Position 3 is /approve for existing work, without overriding tool rules.
Positions 4, 6, 7 and 13 are /next --owner human; their replies give instructions,
not evidence of execution. Position 5 corrects the assumed replacement need.
Positions 8-12 and 17 are /context --kind operator-result, not generic approvals.
Position 8's reported save is contradicted by its reply; position 9's reply
reports a successful check. Position 14 is /explain. Position 15 requests a
/review recommendation on deferral, not unconditional abandonment authority.
Position 16 checks archival eligibility and receives a negative answer; position
17's reply later reports verification. A bare done must therefore retain the
pending-step identity and remain an assertion until the relevant check passes.

## Verification and limits

The four complete retained sequences and paired replies were read in context.
Counts reconcile as 4+3+6+17 inputs and 3+2+5+16 later positions. Source keys,
ordered source lines, complete flags and response-attribution labels were read
from the existing dataset. T01 has one shared opener/answer turn; T02 has one
shared follow-up turn. All T03/T04 positions have native-turn attribution.
These cases do not establish a corpus-wide equivalence rate, independently
validated substitutions, current provider state, or full logical-session closure.
The earlier source-repair and independent-review holds remain with their owners.
