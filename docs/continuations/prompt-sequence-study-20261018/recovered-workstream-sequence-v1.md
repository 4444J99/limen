## Vocabulary and extraction findings

[V5](vocabulary-v5.json) preserves the nineteen V4 contracts and adds two operations:
open, supported by the [artifact sequence](recovered-artifact-sequence-v1.md), and goal here.
The goal contract preserves an explicitly requested native persistent-objective action.
It does not require installing a duplicate command when the harness already owns that control.

A persistent objective is different from ordinary continuation. Its completion evidence is
registration plus readback of the intended objective and conditions; execution and eventual
goal completion are separate. This source contains an explicit request and a later assistant
claim of goal completion, but no historical goal-creation/readback receipt in the returned view.
The study therefore does not assert successful registration or goal completion.

The final planning turn includes a separate native plan item with a detailed implementation
plan. Its short final message is only a concluding action statement. A reader that extracts final
prose alone would miss the plan; a reader that interprets the short final as the entire response
would incorrectly judge the planning request unanswered.

The affirmative at position 9 instead challenges an access denial. By contrast, the assent at
position 12 also introduces substantial new behavior. Neither can safely collapse to a generic
approve token. The requirement-bearing inputs retain their complete payloads; control words
do not replace product specifications.

## Scope and verification

These sixteen cases and the nine artifact-sequence cases total twenty-five new contextual
dispositions across two native views and twenty-seven visible inputs. Their native turn sets
do not overlap each other. They remain separate from previous file-based and recovered-segment
denominators until broader source-origin overlap is reconciled. They are not twenty-five proven
human-authored prompts or successful substitutions.

The existing comparison glossary now displays all twenty-one V5 operations. The structural check
passed after detecting both missing additions. It still renders 382 columns and all 3,500 original
prompt rows, with the six managed recovery inputs labelled. The embedded captured dataset is
byte-identical to its earlier hash. The new source views are represented by the abstract tables
and linked case records, not copied into the raw comparison asset.

Private source bindings, public case positions/counts, unique operation names and unchanged V4
contracts were checked mechanically. Historical project, communication, deployment and completion
claims remain attributed; this study did not independently re-run their underlying predicates.
No browser appearance, complete logical lineage, independent substitution, command installation,
full scoped gate, merge, deployment or session-release success is claimed. The previously filed
host-admission/source-cwd hold and the remaining study acceptance requirements stay open.
