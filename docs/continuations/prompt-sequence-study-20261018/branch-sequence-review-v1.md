## What the branches change

The directory family first chooses condensed chat summaries, then explicitly requests every prompt, response and delivered artifact. The later granularity supersedes that earlier choice. A subsequent continuation inherits the first ten root prompts, excluding the root's unanswered next-step tail. The last continuation excludes the middle file's final repair exchange; its similar request adds connector and Page bindings. A creator-tool invocation has only a provisional workflow target, and the reply asks for clarification without creating a skill.

The handoff family asks for dependency-aware drafts rather than launched agents. The continuation excludes the parent's final table/alphabetization request. Its first next-step question receives a reply reporting verification and publication; those reported effects are not authority supplied by the question alone. Peer completion reports supply context for review, while updated handoffs retain active ownership and accepted-base dependencies.

The naming family includes a repeated clarification whose extended version supplies substantive additional identity facts. Both original screenshots remain embedded in the native source after their temporary paths disappeared. Their inspected labels show 34 distinct children plus the umbrella, with one overlapping label; they do not prove aliases. Later text supplies confirmations and uncertainty. The child branch excludes the parent's four-keyword taxonomy request and reply. Its own added examples yield hyphenated names instead. Those outputs are alternative branch results, not a correction to an inherited response. The manifest plan, proceed instruction and unsuccessful closeout remain separate stages.

The JSON binds original image dimensions, byte counts and short fingerprints without copying images or private prompts.

These are structural and same-coder checks, not independent substitution or complete native-session acceptance.
