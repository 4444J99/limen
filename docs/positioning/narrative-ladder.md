# PSP-P03 narrative ladder

This copy set implements the W03 narrative ladder without changing the accepted
W01 identity contract or W02 audience/disclosure contract. It is a public-safe
content source, not an offer, agreement, price sheet, or publication action.

Use the canonical claims ledger for every evidence-sensitive statement. A claim
that becomes weaker or disputed narrows here immediately.

## L1 — ten seconds

**Production-systems architect.** I build production systems that solve
expensive problems.

For a direct client, I help a named sponsor make a bounded decision about an
active AI or software initiative whose decision rights, verification, cost
boundaries, or handoff are unclear. For a recruiter or hiring executive, I
bring evidence-backed senior systems judgment to a named architecture or
engineering mandate.

The expensive problem is delivery that cannot say who decides, what verified
done means, how cost is bounded, or who owns the system after handoff.

**Strongest proof: Limen.** It is an inspectable governed-delivery system with
operating, failure, and verification evidence in its owner environment.

**Client CTA:** Discuss a fixed-scope Agentic Delivery Audit.

**Recruiter CTA:** Discuss a senior systems architecture or engineering role
with a named mandate.

**Stop rule:** This level identifies role, buyer, expensive problem, strongest
proof, and the appropriate next action. Product-operating partnership is not a
front-door path.

## L2 — five minutes

Complex AI and software initiatives often do not fail because a team lacks
another demonstration. They fail when a delivery system lacks explicit decision
rights, a definition of verified done, bounded cost, evidence for a material
choice, and an owner after the initial intervention ends.

The work is to design those controls around one real initiative. It is not to
replace the buyer's team, take control of the organization, or create permanent
dependency. A written mandate defines authority; current owners remain visible;
the work concludes with evidence, rollback information, and handoff.

Limen is the method proof: a governed delivery system whose public materials
make operating states, failure states, verification, and human gates
inspectable. It supports a claim about method and operating discipline, not a
claim about customer deployment, adoption, revenue, or zero maintenance.

Two supporting proof objects show the method in different product contexts:

- **UCC Public-Records Intelligence Platform:** four implemented state
  collectors sit on a broader architecture. This is bounded implementation
  evidence, not nationwide deployment, customer, or adoption evidence.
- **AI Chat Exporter:** the public product surface presents five export formats.
  This is product-surface evidence, not a usage, revenue, customer, or
  unsupported compatibility claim.

For a direct client, the next decision is whether an evidence-linked,
fixed-scope audit can clarify what to stop, keep, narrow, or govern. For a
recruiter or hiring executive, the next decision is whether the demonstrated
systems judgment fits one named role and mandate. In both cases, the relevant
L1 CTA remains the only public action.

**Stop rule:** A reader can inspect the claims ledger, flagship evidence, and
method boundaries without receiving private diligence material or an implied
commercial commitment.

## L3 — diligence index

L3 is a controlled review layer. It deepens evidence; it does not grant
authority, disclose private sources, or create a public third CTA.

| Diligence area | Review question | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim register and source links | What exact wording, status, limitation, and evidence anchor supports each material claim? | Use `claims-ledger.md` and the accepted flagship evidence; do not promote an inference into a measurement. |
| Method and operating evidence | Can the reader inspect decisions, verification, failure handling, and human gates? | Limen evidence is proof of governed delivery in its owner environment, not client-production or commercial-outcome proof. |
| Scope and authority | What may be inspected, changed, approved, or declined? | A written mandate and named owner are required; diligence access grants no operating authority. |
| Acceptance and handoff | What evidence proves a bounded result, and how does the buyer own it afterward? | Require acceptance evidence, rollback information, temporary-access removal, and a named handoff owner. |
| Economics and contract gates | What must be approved before a commercial commitment? | No public price, rate, discount, equity, licence, revenue-share, custody, or transfer term. `HG-PRICE-ANCHORS` and `HG-CONTRACT` remain human gates. |
| Product-operating partnership | Is one product and operator ready for product-specific diligence? | This path is gated L3 only. Review demand, rights, custody, data, support, operator, and return path; `HG-OPERATOR-TERMS` is required before any terms. |

Private material is shared only through its sanctioned owner, only when needed
for the stated diligence question, and never to substantiate a public claim
without an approved public-safe evidence record.

**Stop rule:** The reader can approve further diligence, request additional
evidence, or decline. Access to this level creates no obligation, authority,
partnership, or transfer.
