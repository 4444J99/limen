## Use notes

Keep the signature plain text and readable without images, tracking pixels or remote assets.
Do not alter reply addresses, account configuration, signatures on other identities, or send a
test email without the required authority. Previewing a draft is not a sent-message receipt.

The brief bio intentionally sends readers to the evidence layer instead of compressing product
counts, rankings or unsupported performance claims into a fixed identity field. Its literal text
must be checked against the actual platform editor before application; this package does not
assume that a remembered platform limit is current.
