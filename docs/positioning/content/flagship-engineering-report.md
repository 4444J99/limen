# A governed multi-agent delivery system: the engineering report

> **Private staged draft — not published, not approved, and not a live report.**

## What this report can say today

Limen is a live orchestration and governance system operating continuously in its owner's environment since May 2026. Here, “production” describes internal operational use; it does not make a customer-deployment claim.

The system is architected and directed by one person through a governed, multi-agent production system. Machine assistance is central to that system and is disclosed as part of the method.

## The operating question

The relevant question is not whether an agent can emit a patch. It is whether work can remain attributable, bounded, verified, reversible, and intelligible when multiple agents and tools operate at once.

The report therefore centers a control loop:

1. A bounded work packet names the scope and authority.
2. A native execution lane produces a receipt tied to an exact revision and predicate.
3. A gate decides whether the evidence is sufficient for the claimed state.
4. Failure, blocking, and rollback remain first-class outcomes rather than exclusions from the story.

## Evidence method

The flagship proof object is designed to let a reader reproduce every headline number from a public endpoint before it contains one. Any eventual metric must include its definition, date range, source endpoint, calculation, caveats, and an independently repeatable check. Failure modes receive equal typographic weight to successful runs.

This staged version intentionally contains **no current count, spend, pass rate, adoption figure, customer claim, or revenue claim**. Those fields remain withheld until independently refreshed and admitted by the claims ledger.

## What the report does not claim

- It does not claim customer deployment, revenue, paying customers, or external adoption.
- It does not claim percentile rankings, “solo-built” authorship, or a volume of shipped products.
- It does not present synthetic test events as observed outcomes.

## Two routes for a future approved surface

- **System audit (`deploy`)** — examine whether authority, verification, receipts, and rollback exist in a delivery system.
- **Builder conversation (`hire`)** — inspect the work and its disclosed method.

These are staged door labels only. The package neither activates capture nor sends any message.
