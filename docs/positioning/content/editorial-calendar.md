# 90-day proof-led editorial calendar — staged

All periods are sequencing windows, not scheduled publication dates. This calendar is the staged deliverable for `PSP-P09-W01`; an asset may advance only after its source remains admitted by `claim-source-register.json`, its prerequisites are complete, and any required human gate has been recorded outside this package.

| Window | PSP work | Audience problem | Staged asset | Canonical source | Door tag | Signal to observe after approved release | Stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | W02 | “Is this operating system real?” | Flagship engineering report draft | `CLM-LIMEN-OPERATING`, `CLM-AUTHORSHIP`, `CLM-PROOF-METHOD` | `deploy` / `hire` | attributable inbound initiated from the report | No owner identity approval, no release. |
| Weeks 3–4 | W03 | “Where does agentic delivery fail expensively?” | Failure-modes essay | W02 source set plus admitted method claims | `deploy` | qualified diagnostic conversation | A mode without evidence stays a labeled hypothesis. |
| Weeks 5–6 | W04 | “Why is token or commit volume an insufficient metric?” | Control-economics essay and reproducible metric definition | `CLM-PROOF-METHOD` | `deploy` | request for the metric method or audit | No chart or number is released without a reproduced method. |
| Weeks 7–8 | W05 | “How do authority and verification compose?” | Delivery-gates walkthrough | `CLM-PROOF-METHOD`, `CLM-AUTHORSHIP` | `deploy` | walkthrough-to-door attribution | Any private example is replaced with the synthetic example. |
| Weeks 9–10 | W06 | “Does the system acknowledge and correct failure?” | Sanitized case-study shell | `WITHHELD-INCIDENT` | `deploy` | correction review request, not a performance claim | No real incident is substituted without review. |

| Any approved window | W08 | “Can a relevant reader reach the proof safely?” | Human-operated distribution run sheet | W07 canonical source and `CLM-CAPTURE-DOORS` | per-channel tag | attributable visit or inbound, measured separately | No send without `HG-PUBLICATION-SEND`; stop a channel on claim drift or zero qualified signal. |

## Calendar integrity rules

1. Every asset has exactly one canonical proof source and one audience problem.
2. `deploy` and `hire` are door classifications, not evidence that capture is active or a response occurred.
3. A lack of signal is information: pause that asset/channel rather than increase volume or make a stronger claim.
4. The calendar contains no promise of cadence, reach, conversion, revenue, or external adoption.
