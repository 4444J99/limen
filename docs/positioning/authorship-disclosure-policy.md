# Authorship & Verification Disclosure Policy

How machine-assisted authorship is described on every public surface. Machine assistance is not
an embarrassment to conceal; it is central to the system thesis — and it must be described
precisely. Companion to `docs/positioning/claims-ledger.md`.

## The canonical sentence

> Architected and directed by one person through a governed, multi-agent production system.

This is the only approved substitute for "solo-built." Never imply every line was manually
authored; never obscure that one person owns the architecture, direction, verification gates,
and acceptance decisions.

## Terminology guide

| Say | Never say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Architected and directed by one person through a governed, multi-agent production system" | "Solo-built" (as every-line-manual) | Precise; the governance is the thesis |
| "Agent-generated under my architecture and direction, merged through verification gates" | "AI wrote it" / hiding assistance | Both self-diminishment and concealment are false |
| "N tests, per the repository's CI" (dated) | "battle-tested", "production-grade" (bare) | Numbers over adjectives; assertions carry their source |
| "Operating continuously in my environment since <date>" | "In production" (implying customers) | Internal operation ≠ customer deployment |
| "Four state collectors implemented on a fifty-state architecture" | "All 50 states" | Implemented vs designed |
| "313 repositories (dated census)" | "~100 shipped products" | Repos ≠ products; volume is context, not the lead |

## Authorship classes (per-repository disclosure)

Every flagship or case-study repository states one of:

1. **Original** — manually authored.
2. **Agent-directed** — generated by agents under the owner's architecture, prompts, gates, and
   review; merged only through verification.
3. **Substantially transformed fork** — upstream named; transformation described.
4. **Light fork** — upstream named; kept for utility, not claimed as original work.
5. **Human collaboration** — collaborators named with roles.
6. **Infrastructure / mirror / template / archive** — support material, not product.

## Verification-claim rules

- A metric appears publicly only with: its **source** (whose CI, which dashboard), its **date**,
  and — where machine-assistance is relevant — its treatment of machine-generated commits.
- Contribution totals are internal-only unless accompanied by methodology, date range,
  machine-assist treatment, and reproducible source data.
- Percentile/ranking claims require an independent, reproducible external source. None currently
  exists; none are published (see ledger never-publish list).
- Liveness claims require a dated liveness receipt (HTTP 200 / health endpoint / public
  dashboard), not a platform's "built" status.

## Why this is the strong position

The governed production system is the differentiator. A buyer who discovers the work is
machine-assisted after an implied manual claim loses all trust; a buyer told up front that one
person directs a verified multi-agent fleet — with receipts — is looking at the product itself.
The disclosure *is* the demonstration.
