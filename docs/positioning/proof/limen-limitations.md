# Limen — Limitations and Withheld Claims

This case study describes inspectable mechanisms and September 8, 2026 observations. It does not certify the
system or prove that every historical agent action followed its protocol.

- **Correctness is conditional.** Tests exercise specified behavior. Incomplete predicates,
  selection gaps and invalid receipts can still admit wrong conclusions.
- **Controls have failed in practice.** Forged completion receipts and misleading phase-closure
  documents required correction. Preserve those failures in any evaluation of reliability.
- **Runtime access was limited in the recorded session.** The September 8 session could not obtain a configured broker
  capability response. That observation neither demonstrates autonomous availability nor diagnoses
  every deployment as unavailable.
- **Runtime and test outcomes must remain distinct.** An early R00 heavy verification attempt was
  denied by host admission. The later September 8 checkpoint obtained admission and executed the
  suites, with CLI failures still unresolved. Passing shards, a changed host condition and a
  complete green verification run are different claims. E08 records the chronology.
- **Parallelism needs ownership.** Isolated checkouts reduce collisions but do not automatically
  prevent competing owners, incorrect cleanup or stale handoffs.
- **Measured business effectiveness is not established here.** There is no sourced cost saving,
  revenue improvement, adoption rate, reliability percentage or controlled alternative comparison.
- **External evidence cannot be synthesized into existence.** Reader participation, sponsor
  acceptance, publication consent and operator transfer require actual observations and authority.
- **Scope is the owner's environment and the cited source versions.** Broader deployment,
  maintainability, security and operational suitability require their own evidence.
- **Publication remains a separate decision.** The independent factual/publication verdict belongs
  to P05-W01. Even a publishable verdict does not grant authority to distribute the report, certify
  its commercial effectiveness or satisfy the task's completion predicate by itself.

Do not describe Limen as guaranteeing high-quality outcomes, eliminating infinite loops, replacing
human judgment or proving that all probabilistic agents can safely deliver complex systems. Those
claims exceed the evidence in this package.
