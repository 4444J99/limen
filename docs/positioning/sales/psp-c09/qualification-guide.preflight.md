## Ideal client

A technical or product leader owns one material agentic/software delivery decision. The initiative
is bounded, read-only evidence can be inspected, a decision window exists, and an internal owner can
receive the result. The Audit is useful only if the sponsor is willing to keep, kill, narrow, or
govern based on evidence.

## Evidence, not vibes

- A “trigger” counts only when a decision/date, bounded scope, source, and uncertainty are recorded.
- Failure cost may be qualitative or banded; no unsourced total is invented.
- Authority is a role and decision right, never an inferred title.
- Read-only evidence is an availability signal, not permission to ingest it.
- Missing required evidence routes to one bounded follow-up or decline, never indefinite nurture.

## Disqualification

No sponsor, unbounded scope, emergency/on-call demand, organization takeover, outcome guarantee,
security/approval bypass, or required production-write access is terminal for this route. Legal,
pricing, regulated-data, account/custody, or public-claim exceptions route to human review rather
than being “handled” in a sales conversation.

Ten fictional accounts exercise qualified, follow-up, and decline routes with explicit evidence and
uncertainty. No company, person, contact detail, live deal, paid outcome, or fabricated market result
appears in the fixture.
