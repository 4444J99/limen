# Client narrative and problem map

## Client narrative (J-C1 · L1)

**Before state:** An active AI or software initiative can be busy and still be
stalled. No one can show who decides the next material question, what evidence
closes it, how work stays within a cost boundary, or who owns the system at
handoff. The sponsor pays for motion while delivery and governance risk
accumulate without a defensible decision.

**Bounded intervention:** A fixed-scope Agentic Delivery Audit examines one
named sponsor, one active initiative, and one decision. It maps decision
rights, verification, cost boundaries, evidence, and handoff; it does not
expand into a general transformation program.

**Expected decision:** The sponsor receives an evidence-linked choice to stop,
keep, narrow, or govern the initiative, plus the minimum control design needed
for the chosen path. This is a bounded decision, not a promise of a delivery
result.

**Exact evidence anchor:** [Limen public status snapshot](https://limen-dashboard.pages.dev/public-status.json)
reported 3,111 total tasks and 1,357 completed tasks on 2026-08-10. It is
public operational evidence for governed delivery in its owner's environment,
not evidence of external commercial results.

## Problem taxonomy (J-C1 · L2)

| Problem | Costly condition | Bounded question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision-rights gap | Work waits or reverses because the sponsor, approver, and escalation path are unclear. | Who decides the next material question, on what evidence? |
| Verification gap | Progress is reported, but nobody can show what verified done means. | What observable evidence closes the decision? |
| Cost-boundary gap | Work can continue without a stated limit, trade-off, or stop condition. | What must be bounded, narrowed, or stopped? |
| Handoff gap | A change may appear complete while its ongoing owner, operating record, and return path are absent. | Who can operate and own the result after the intervention? |

## Outcome language (J-C1 · L2)

Use decision language, not an outcome guarantee:

- **Stop:** The evidence does not support continuing the initiative as scoped.
- **Keep:** The current path has a named decision owner, credible verification,
  and a bounded next step.
- **Narrow:** Retain only the initiative portion that can be owned, verified,
  and bounded.
- **Govern:** Add the minimum decision, verification, cost, evidence, and
  handoff controls before further delivery.
